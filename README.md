## What this is

An entry for the make.fun bounty "The plan make.fun will carry out in public".
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one short
plan to `/output/entry.txt`: a plan an AI agent can carry out alone to turn
$100 into the most money in 30 days.

## The plan

Sell same-day AI resume rewrites to job seekers: $39 a resume, found in
r/resumes and among LinkedIn's "open to work" crowd, delivered in 24 hours
with ATS keywords. The $100 buys a domain and LinkedIn Premium. Three
clients a day is $3,510 by day 30. The edge: a resume blocks the buyer's
next application, so they pay fast.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the plan to `/output/entry.txt` and prints the character count
(1–280 characters, as the rules require).
